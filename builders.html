Common questions

Questions from builders & insurance repairers

Do you work directly with insurance repair companies, not just builders?

Yes. We take on subcontract maintenance and repair scopes for insurance repair companies as well as builders - the work is the same discipline: strip-back, make-good, minor works, done to a brief and signed off properly.

Can you take on a standing subcontract arrangement across multiple sites?

Yes - many builders and insurance repair companies use us as their go-to subcontractor for defect and make-good work across several active sites at once, rather than re-briefing a new trade each time.

What size of punchlist or defect list do you take on?

Anything from a handful of items to a full post-handover list. See our full range of services or send through your list for a quote.
