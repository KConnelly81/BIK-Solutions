How we work

Built for ongoing maintenance, not just one-off jobs

Most of our work starts as a single job and turns into an ongoing arrangement - a property manager with a rolling list of make-goods, a strata scheme with recurring common-area repairs, or a builder who wants one subcontractor for every site's small works. If that's what you're after, say so when you get in touch. We're just as happy quoting a single repair as we are setting up a standing maintenance arrangement across a portfolio.

On site

The detail is the job.

A patched wall that disappears into the paint. A door that closes properly again. Skirting that lines up. It's the small, close-up finishing detail that separates a make-good from a rushed job — and it's what we photograph on every site.

Common questions

Questions about our services

Do you take on ongoing or scheduled maintenance, or only one-off jobs?

Both. Many of our property manager, strata and commercial clients start with a single job and move to a standing maintenance arrangement once they see how we work. There's no minimum job size or contract requirement to get started.

What areas do you cover?

Gold Coast is our home base, including suburbs such as Southport, Robina, Burleigh Heads, Broadbeach, Varsity Lakes and Helensvale. We also cover Logan and southern Brisbane. See our full service area or ask us directly if you're not sure.

Can you manage maintenance across multiple properties or sites?

Yes - this is common for body corporates, real estate agencies with rental portfolios, and commercial clients with more than one site. One point of contact, consistent quality across every property.

Do you work with builders and insurance repair companies as a subcontractor?

Yes. See our builders & insurance repair page for defect rectification, punchlist completion and subcontract maintenance support.
